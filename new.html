Arul Anandar College is an arts and sciences autonomous college affiliated to Madurai Kamaraj University, situated at Karumathur, Madurai, and administered by the Jesuits.

The College NAME, meaning "filled with grace", refers to john de Britto, the Jesuit saint martyred at Orur, tamilnadu, India.
